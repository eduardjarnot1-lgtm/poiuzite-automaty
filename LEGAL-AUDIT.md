# Legal, privacy, accessibility and consumer-information audit

Audit date: 2026-11-06. Scope: this repository (branch built on PR #5) and the preview built from it. This is an engineering audit, not legal advice. It does not make the site legally compliant, approved or risk-free. The legal pages are **drafts** and need business facts and a review by a qualified Czech lawyer before they are published as final.

## What the site actually is (verified in the code and in a browser)

- Static Czech catalogue generated by `scripts/build.mjs`, deployed (per `netlify.toml`) on Netlify. No live URL is configured (`site.baseUrl` is empty), so a live deployment was **not** inspected.
- **Enquiry only.** One form (`poptavka`) on the contact, category, product, refurbishment, parts and service pages. Fields: name, e-mail and message (required); company and phone (optional); hidden page type and product. It posts to Netlify Forms (or `forms.endpoint`). There is no cart, no binding order, no account and no payment.
- **No cookies, localStorage, sessionStorage or IndexedDB** on the public build (checked on 14 pages in Chromium, fresh profile).
- **No analytics, pixels, session replay, ads, maps, social embeds or third-party video.** Videos are self-hosted MP4/WebM.
- Before this change the only third-party request on every page was **Google Fonts** (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), which passes the visitor's IP address to Google. Now self-hosted: **0 third-party requests** on all 14 pages.
- The preview build (`--prototype`) adds a browser-only catalogue manager that stores data in the viewer's own localStorage/IndexedDB. It is never in the Netlify build, has no login and cannot change what other visitors see.
- The claude.ai artifact host and its toolbar are outside this application. Anything they load or store is controlled by the platform, not by this site, and is not described in the site's policies.

## Business information still needed (priority order)

1. Legal name and form (s.r.o. / OSVČ), IČO, registered office, register entry (court, section, insert, or živnostenský rejstřík).
2. Customers: businesses only, consumers too, or both. VAT payer yes/no and DIČ.
3. How a contract is formed after an enquiry (e-mail confirmation, signed contract, at collection) and whether consumers usually buy entirely at a distance.
4. Customer e-mail and phone; complaints/returns address if different; contact for personal-data requests.
5. Defect period for used machines (statutory 24 months; used goods may be shortened by agreement to not less than one year), any extra warranty, any voluntary returns.
6. Delivery, installation, collection and servicing actually offered, area and pricing; estimated cost of returning a machine after withdrawal.
7. Live hosting (Netlify?) and live URL; e-mail provider receiving enquiries; how long enquiries are kept.
8. Rights evidence for the product photos, the PixVerse videos (plan and terms), the background videos and the wave images.
9. Whether every machine really is checked, cleaned, repaired and tested before sale.

## Release blockers (prioritised)

1. Operator identity and contacts (finding 2). The build prints this list; `node scripts/build.mjs --strict` fails until it is empty.
2. Product photos without permission (finding 5): hidden from the public build until cleared or replaced.
3. Legal pages are drafts (`legal.status: "draft"`): need the facts above and a Czech lawyer's review, then set `"final"` and `updated`.
4. Customer type and VAT status, which decide price presentation and which consumer rules apply (findings 10, 11).
5. Rights for the videos and background images (finding 6).
6. Confirmation of the inspection/refurbishment claims and condition descriptions (findings 12, 13).
7. Confirm hosting, form provider, e-mail provider and retention (finding 3).

## Where qualified Czech legal review is still needed

Terms and complaint page wording; whether contracts are distance contracts in practice; shortened defect period for used goods and the §2167 exclusions; the current model withdrawal form; B2B terms; retention periods; whether the accessibility act applies; discount presentation if prices are shown to consumers.
