# Terminologický slovníček / Terminologie / Terminologický slovník

Czech (cs) is the source. German (de) and Slovak (sk) follow this list in every text, page and
product translation. German uses the polite "Sie", Slovak "vykanie". Machine names, brand names,
models, the business name "Použité automaty", "Bistro 24", e-mail addresses, phone numbers, URLs,
IDs and slugs are never translated.

## What the translations must not add

No international shipping, no German- or Slovak-speaking support, no service outside what the
Czech text says, no warranties, payment methods, response times, country prices or products.
Nothing in German suggests an office, a branch or a service network in Germany or Austria, and
nothing in Slovak suggests one in Slovakia. Bistro 24 links point to https://bistro24.eu/, whose
pages may be in Czech.
